The Challenge

Scaling a BD team from scratch

When I stepped into the BD Lead role at AHA Commerce, the team had no structured acquisition process, no consistent KPIs, and no visibility into pipeline health. My mandate: build a repeatable system that a team of 8 could run at scale — and hit aggressive targets across Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Tokopedia.

The Process

7-stage acquisition funnel, rebuilt end-to-end

01
Prospecting & Research
Systematic ICP definition and market scan across e-commerce verticals
02

AI-powered outreach sequences cutting manual prospecting time by 150%

The Approach

Three levers that moved the needle

⚙️
Data-Driven Qualification
Introduced a structured scoring framework to qualify leads by GMV potential, platform fit, and partnership readiness — eliminating low-probability deals early and focusing effort on high-converting prospects.
🤖
AI & Workflow Automation
Deployed n8n and Zapier-powered outreach automation and built WebApp dashboards for pipeline visibility. Reduced manual admin time by 150%, allowing the team to focus on relationship-building and closing.
👥
Team & Culture Alignment
Led a BD team of 8 with individual KPIs, weekly 1-on-1s, and performance dashboards. Built a results-oriented culture that reduced churn and improved quota attainment consistently quarter-over-quarter.
Team Achievement

From zero structured process to a high-output acquisition machine — the team closed 47 brand partnerships in 9 months, spanning fashion, lifestyle, F&B, sports, and beauty across three e-commerce platforms.
